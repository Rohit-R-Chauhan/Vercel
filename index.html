About Rohit R Chauhan

Rohit R Chauhan is an entrepreneur and financial innovator focused on building smarter and more practical investing systems for modern investors. As the Founder of InGood, he is known for introducing the idea of adaptive investing through the concept of the Self-Adjusting SIP.

His work is driven by a deep understanding of how people actually manage money in real life, especially in situations where income, expenses, and financial priorities constantly change.

Rohit R Chauhan’s approach to finance is strongly influenced by behavioral investing and cash flow management. He believes many people struggle with investing not because they lack financial goals, but because traditional investment systems are often too rigid and disconnected from real-world financial behavior.

Instead of promoting fixed commitments and financial pressure, he focuses on creating systems that support flexibility, liquidity, and long-term consistency.

Vision & Innovation

Before building InGood, Rohit spent time studying common financial patterns among salaried professionals, freelancers, young earners, and first-time investors.

He observed that unpredictable expenses, irregular income, and fear of losing liquidity were major reasons why people paused or avoided investing altogether.

This insight inspired him to create a platform that adapts according to affordability rather than forcing users into uncomfortable financial commitments.

Cash Flow Investing

Investment systems designed around real monthly income, affordability, and practical spending behavior.

Self-Adjusting SIP

Smart SIP structures that automatically adapt according to changing financial conditions and surplus funds.

Liquidity First

Flexible investing focused on financial comfort and maintaining healthy cash flow instead of rigid commitments.

Financial Automation

Intelligent automation that reduces financial stress and helps users build sustainable investment habits over time.

Future of Adaptive Investing

Through the InGood App, Rohit R Chauhan introduced features such as cash flow–based investing, automatic surplus detection, self-adjusting contributions, liquidity-first planning, and intelligent automation.

Powered by InGoodfinserv, the platform is designed to reduce financial stress while helping users build more sustainable investment habits over time.

Beyond product development, Rohit R Chauhan regularly shares insights on financial psychology, investment consistency, saving behavior, and the future of adaptive investing.

His long-term vision is to make investing more accessible, human-centered, and practical for a generation that values flexibility and financial confidence over rigid financial systems.
